# Contributing

Contributions are welcome when they preserve the project's technical,
licensing, privacy, and attribution boundaries.

- External contributions do not change the historical fact that Ting
  (peanutsuee) is the original concept creator and project initiator.
- Contributors retain appropriate authorship credit for their contributions.
- Contributions are distributed under the license applicable to the affected
  files.
- Contributions must preserve applicable upstream MIT notices and file-level
  provenance.
- Changes that remove, hide, or intentionally weaken lawful original or
  upstream attribution will not be accepted.
- Brand changes must not present an unauthorized fork as an official version.

The project does not currently claim a Contributor License Agreement or
Developer Certificate of Origin signing process.
